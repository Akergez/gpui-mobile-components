Intent design system for aiddiction, matterfast and money-manager

Screens are written once against intents: what the person is doing, not which widget to show. An intent renders twice: in the Pointer skin, which is gpui-kit following its Design Guides, and in the Touch skin, which is Material 3 with motion. The colours of both skins are the existing engine's: gpui-adaptive-colors (a scheme from one seed, or a Zed theme the person wears). Everything a phone needs beyond gpui-mobile is gpui-android-glue.

Rules

  • Feature code never asks for the platform. The one escape hatch is Form::of(window, cx).is_touch(), and every use of it in a feature gets reviewed.
  • Colour is read from cx.theme(). cx.scheme() roles such as secondary_container and the surface steps add to it but always fall back to the theme, because a worn Zed theme replaces the theme and leaves the scheme behind.
  • Every way of input stays available in both skins: typing a date and picking it, a shortcut and a button, a gesture and a menu.
  • Every scenario runs twice: Pointer at 1280×800 and Touch at 400×800 with swipes. GPUI_KEYBOARD=300 pretends a keyboard is up.
