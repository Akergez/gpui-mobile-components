Destinations: the app's sections

The app's top-level sections, with a badge for what waits on the person. There can be an optional account or server switcher at the top and pinned items at the bottom, such as Settings. Selecting a destination resets the Navigator to that section's root.

aiddiction: Inbox · Projects · Settings, server switchermoney-manager: Records · Categories · Charts · Accounts, account switchermatterfast: team switcher; the channel list is the root page
Destinations::new("sections", &self.navigator)
    .switcher(Switcher::new("server", server.name(), server.status()).on_open(open_servers))
    .destination(Destination::new(Section::Inbox, "Inbox", IconName::Inbox).badge(waiting))
    .destination(Destination::new(Section::Projects, "Projects", IconName::Folder))
    .pinned(Destination::new(Section::Settings, "Settings", IconName::Settings))
    .primary(Command::new("new-chat", "New chat", IconName::SquarePen, NewChat))
