Screen: a page with a title and commands

A page has a title, an optional subtitle, an optional "up", one primary command and secondary commands. Its content is one scroll area, which the Screen owns so it can lock the axis and collapse the app bar. aiddiction already draws this by hand twice, phone and desktop, in shell.rs::render_screen and bar_actions.

    .command(Command::new("rename", "Rename…", IconName::Pencil, RenameProject).overflow())
