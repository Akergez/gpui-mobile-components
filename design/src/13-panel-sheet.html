Panel, Sheet, Dock

Panel: secondary content beside the page, such as a thread, the inbox, search results or an inspector. It is a third column or an overlay on Pointer and a pushed page or a tall sheet on Touch. Sheet: a short task or a set of choices over the page. On Touch this is money-manager's bottom_sheet.rs, generalised: it can be stacked, dragged by its handle, closed by the scrim and by back. Dock: a persistent strip for something that outlives the page, matterfast's call dock.
