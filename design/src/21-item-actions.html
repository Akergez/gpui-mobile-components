ItemActions: what can be done to one item

Commands bound to one item: a message, a channel row, a record, a task. matterfast already has both halves. On Pointer, hover_actions.rs shows a small bar over the hovered row, and right-click opens post_menu.rs. On Touch, post_sheet.rs opens on long press: quick reactions, the bar's actions and the ⋯ menu in one sheet, plus "Copy text", because the long press is taken before text selection. Rows can also carry swipe actions on Touch. Several items can be acted on at once in selection mode.

Collection::new("records", &self.records)
    .item_actions(|record| ItemActions::new()
        .quick(Command::new(("edit", record.id), "Edit", IconName::Pencil, EditRecord(record.id)))
        .command(Command::new(("dup", record.id), "Duplicate", IconName::Copy, Duplicate(record.id)))
        .command(Command::new(("del", record.id), "Delete", IconName::Trash, Delete(record.id)).destructive())
        .swipe_end(SwipeAction::destructive(Delete(record.id))))
    .selectable(true)
