Confirm, Ask, Undo

Confirm asks before something that cannot be undone: leave a channel, archive it, remove a member, delete an account. Ask offers a handful of answers, one of which is chosen. This is matterfast's dialogs/choose.rs, and also aiddiction's permission request when an agent asks to write a file, which appears inline in the feed and not as a modal. Undo replaces confirmation for reversible actions, as the Coding Guides require: "no confirmation dialogs for reversible, low-risk actions".

matterfast: confirm_leave, confirm_archive, confirm_remove_member, chooseaiddiction: permission requestsmoney-manager: delete record → Undo
Confirm::new("leave", "Leave #design-crit?")
    .body("You will stop getting its messages…")
    .confirm(Command::new("leave-yes", "Leave channel", IconName::LogOut, LeaveChannel(id)).destructive())
    .open(window, cx);            // Pointer: AlertDialog · Touch: M3 dialog; both a back layer

Ask::new(("permission", request.id), "Write hello.txt")
    .answer("allow", "Allow", Allow(request.id)).answer("reject", "Reject", Reject(request.id))
    .inline()                     // in the feed, not modal (aiddiction); .open() for a dialog (matterfast)

Undo::notice("Record deleted", Restore(record.id)).push(window, cx);
  • A confirm button names the action ("Leave channel"), never "OK". The destructive answer is never the Enter default.
  • Ask with more than 3 answers becomes a list: radio rows in the dialog on Pointer, a sheet of rows on Touch.
  • Undo lives 6 s on Touch (snackbar) and until dismissed or replaced on Pointer, and Ctrl Z works while it is shown.
