Form: fields, validation, one commitment

A titled set of fields with Cancel and one commitment. All three apps have one: aiddiction's ui/forms.rs (a dialog padded by the keyboard), matterfast's dialogs/forms/* (Mattermost interactive dialogs built from a spec) and money-manager's open_form, which on a phone already opens every form in a bottom sheet. Each field declares its Purpose, and that purpose sets the phone's keyboard and its action key.

Form::new("new-expense", "New expense")
    .field(Field::amount("amount", &self.amount).label("Amount").currency(account.currency()))
    .field(Field::date("date", &self.date).label("Date"))
    .field(Field::choice("category", &self.category).label("Category"))
    .field(Field::text("comment", &self.comment).label("Comment").optional())
    .commit(Command::new("add", "Add expense", IconName::Check, AddRecord))
    .open(window, cx);
