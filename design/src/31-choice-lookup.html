Choice, Lookup, Segments, Toggle

Choice picks one item from a known list: project, agent, theme, category, account. Lookup picks from a directory too large to list, such as people or channels, where the caller answers each query. That is matterfast's dialogs/picker.rs (set_results). Segments covers 2–4 exclusive modes, such as money-manager's expense/income switch. Toggle is on/off.
