DateField, Amount

money-manager's date_field.rs is the model for both skins. The date is a line of text first, because typing a date takes four or eight key presses while picking one takes a dozen taps. The calendar is one button away and stays in step with the text. It is laid out in rems so it still fits at a larger zoom. Amount is a number in a currency: a numeric keyboard, grouping, a sign that follows the record's kind, and a large figure on a phone.

money-manager: record and transfer dialogs (two amounts, rate derived)matterfast: schedule message, post reminder
DateField::new(&self.date).label("Date")          // DateFieldState: text + calendar, one value
Amount::new(&self.amount).currency(Currency::RUB).sign(Sign::Expense)
  • DateField replaces gpui-kit's DatePicker, which can only be clicked and whose calendar is laid out in pixels. The calendar is gpui-kit Calendar sized in rems on Pointer, and the M3 date picker (modal, with a pencil to switch to input) on Touch.
  • The keyboard purpose is Number for both fields, and dates accept 12.10, 12.10.26 or 2026-10-12 on both skins.
  • Amount: tabular figures, grouping by locale, and the sign comes from Segments (expense/income) rather than from typing a minus.
