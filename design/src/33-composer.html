Composer: writing a message to someone, or to an agent

A growing text field with attachments, completion (@mentions, ~channels, :emoji:, /commands) and send. Options can sit around it: aiddiction's agent mode ("Ask first") and the plan strip, matterfast's priority, schedule and paste-an-image. On Touch it rides on the keyboard, its completion list opens above it, and the tools collapse into a "+" that opens a sheet.

matterfast: chat/render/composer.rs, completion_list.rs, mentions/*aiddiction: chat.rs (mode, commands, plan, permissions)
Composer::new(&self.draft)                               // ComposerState: text, attachments, completion
    .completion(Completion::mentions(&directory).channels().emoji().commands(&commands))
    .tool(Command::new("attach", "Attach files", IconName::Paperclip, Attach))
    .tool(Command::new("priority", "Priority", IconName::CircleAlert, SetPriority))
    .tool(Command::new("schedule", "Schedule…", IconName::Clock, Schedule))
    .above(plan_strip)                                    // aiddiction's plan / matterfast's reply-to
    .send(Command::new("send", "Send", IconName::Send, Send))
  • Touch: padded by bars.max(keyboard_inset(window)), and the keyboard purpose is Message. Enter inserts a newline and the send button sends. Paste goes through clipboard::pull, and images from the clipboard become attachments.
  • Pointer: Enter sends, Shift Enter breaks the line, ↑ in an empty field edits the last message, and completion is driven by the keyboard.
  • The draft is per channel or conversation and persists, as matterfast rhs/draft.rs already does.
