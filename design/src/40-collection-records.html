Collection, Records

Collection is a virtualised list of items with stable ids, optional sections such as Favourites, Channels and Direct messages, or the inbox's Needs you / Working / Done groups, and per-item actions. Records is tabular data that is a sortable DataTable when the window is wide enough and a list of rows with the key fields when it is not. money-manager does exactly this today with its records.
