Board, Review, Chart

Three heavier surfaces, each owned by one app but built from the same rules. Board: aiddiction's task board, with To do, In progress and Done. Review: aiddiction's diffreview-ui, where you annotate lines and send the notes to the agent. Chart: money-manager's pie by category, bars by month and the running total.
