Notice, Banner, Progress, Empty, Identity

Notice: a short message that goes away by itself, optionally with one action. Banner: a standing state of the page, such as a call in progress, offline, sign-in needed or a sync conflict. Progress: work that is pending, either determinate or not. Empty: nothing to show yet, plus the one thing to do about it. Identity: a face with presence, an unread badge or a status dot. matterfast's kit.rs exists "so that the same thing looks the same in the sidebar, the feed and the inbox".
